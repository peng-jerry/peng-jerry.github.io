About Me

Hello! I am Jerry Peng, a current masters in Mechanical Engineering student at Carnegie Mellon University with a concentration area in Robotic & Control Systems.

I received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Pittsburgh.

I’m currently part of the Robomechanics Lab, working under Aaron Johnson on Unmanned Surface Vehicles (USVs).

I enjoy hands-on engineering work and am seeking entry-level mechanical engineering roles in mechanical design, mechatronics, controls, and robotics!

Take a look at my projects under the projects tab, and feel free to reach out by email if you’d like to chat about any opportunities.
